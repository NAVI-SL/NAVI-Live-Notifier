## Stop and recovery rules

Stop the actual owning worker on an unexpected target/mention, duplicate normal alert, invalid signature acceptance, suspected secret exposure, DB/ledger loss or unresolved delivery uncertainty. Preserve records/logs; inspect actual Discord before retry. Never delete sent/uncertain rows, remove existing volumes, bulk replay or change shared auth encryption keys. Use the [rollback plan](ROLLBACK_PLAN.md) and pause until the owner reviews a safe resume.

Do not deliberately disconnect a real sender during an approved actual send without separately reviewed failure scope; use existing synthetic/SQL recovery fixtures first. The successful local backup restore does not validate replay reconciliation after a live rollback, encrypted offsite protection, or all deletion exclusions.

## Acceptance and decision

Require independently observed correct new-start notifications for **both** platforms, exact counts for approved destinations, no unauthorized mention/access, repeat/restart deduplication, saved settings persistence and measured discovery/processing/receipt times. Record failures and API/indexing/Premiere/short-stream limitations. A small two-source experiment is not measured public capacity.

All actual livestream rows remain **BLOCKED pending exact target/send approval and actual process/consent verification**. Public release stays NO-GO even if local polling later passes, until policy/contact/deletion, HTTPS/webhooks, alerting/soak, hosting capacity and recovery gates close. No invitations, actual messages, public installation or next phase is authorized by this plan.
