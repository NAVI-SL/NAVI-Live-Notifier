## Service and access

NAVI provides public YouTube/Kick channel registration and Discord livestream-start notifications, controlled through a dashboard. The next intended stage is a restricted **private beta**, maximum ten approved Discord servers/ten unique platform accounts globally, subject to actual validation. These limits are planning bounds, not a capacity or uptime guarantee. General public access, paid services, scheduled reminders, offline alerts/edits and additional platforms are not offered by this preparation.

Only approved accounts may currently log in. To manage a server you must be its owner or hold current Administrator/Manage Server permission and be entitled to configure its notification destination. Do not use another user's session or misrepresent authority. Installing a bot does not itself grant dashboard rights. Revocation can prevent further configuration; an already initiated message cannot be recalled.

Before use the final published Terms and Privacy Policy must be accessible, reviewed and accepted through the approved user flow. ## Acceptable use and configuration responsibility

Use only for servers/accounts you are allowed to configure, follow platform rules, and respect channel members. Do not spam, harass, impersonate, circumvent quotas/access controls, send forged webhooks, probe unauthorized servers or upload secret/illegal material in templates. Do not exploit notifications for unwanted mass mentions.

Choose an appropriate destination and template. Everyone mentions require a deliberate saved selection and effective bot permission; No mention is the initial safe choice. Test notifications are real labelled messages, may mention a selected normal role, and must be used only in approved destinations. Preview/test messages never ping everyone. Inspect Discord before retrying an uncertain send. Do not request clearing delivery history to force duplicates.

## Platform dependencies and reliability

NAVI depends on Discord, YouTube, Kick, hosting and network availability. API quotas, permissions, indexing delays and outages can delay or prevent alerts. Registering or enabling during an already-live broadcast does not backfill it. Notification settings/worker must be active before a new eligible start. Local sleep/shutdown stops monitoring. No exactly-once delivery guarantee, public SLA or reliable Premiere exclusion is established. The unresolved YouTube candidate-starvation defect and real acceptance gaps must be disclosed and corrected before release claims.

NAVI does not represent endorsement by those services. Users retain applicable rights in their content; NAVI should only process/display what is necessary to provide authorized notifications.
